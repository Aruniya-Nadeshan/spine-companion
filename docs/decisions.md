# Decisions

## 2026-11-19: Defer the NHS as a source
Paraphrasing NHS text counts as adaptation under their terms, and adapted content can't be attributed to the NHS. That conflicts with citing sources. v1 uses NIAMS and SOSORT.

## 2026-11-19: Raw and processed data stay out of git
Avoids redistributing licensed content; the repo holds the recipe (sources.csv plus ingestion code).
