# spine-companion

A supportive chatbot that helps people with scoliosis understand their condition, in plain language and from trustworthy sources.

> ⚠️ **Not medical advice.** This project provides general educational information only. It cannot diagnose, treat, or replace guidance from your doctor, spinal specialist, or physiotherapist. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your own situation.

## What is this project?

Living with scoliosis can bring a lot of questions, and finding clear, reliable answers is often hard. This project is an early-stage chatbot that answers common questions about scoliosis, such as what it is, how it is monitored, and what treatment options exist. It is designed to answer from vetted medical sources rather than guess.

The long-term goal is to grow this into a research project on how AI can support patients with scoliosis safely and compassionately.

## Who is it for?

- People recently diagnosed with scoliosis who want to understand what it means
- Teenagers and adults living with scoliosis, and the families supporting them
- Anyone looking for calm, easy-to-read information before or after a medical appointment

## Status

🚧 Early development. Not ready for real use yet.
